# Rules for working on this project

Read this before changing anything. These are business rules set by the owner, Morten. Do not change them without his written instruction.

## Money and pricing
- All figures in USD.
- Client price = (operator price + direct trip costs) ÷ 0.91, rounded up. Archē's margin is 9% of the price the client pays, as a rule (Morten, 25 Oct 2026; was 7%). 9% is the minimum; a different margin on a trip, higher or lower, only in special circumstances and only by Morten's decision, with the reason recorded (Morten, 29 Oct 2026).
- Deposit 25% of the charter price (at least USD 2,500) on signature; balance 7 days before the first departure; full payment if departure is under 7 days away. Fixed-price extras are added to the balance; items at cost are invoiced after the flight with receipts, without margin. If the operator wants money earlier, or its cancellation charges rise earlier, the client pays earlier: each confirmation prints its own payment schedule (A3) (Morten, 29 Oct 2026).
- Archē never pre-funds: the operator is paid only from cleared client money, never more than has been received, and never more than the operator price; Archē keeps its margin. The operator's contract is signed only once the client has signed and paid what the operator wants at that point. At every moment the client money held covers what Archē would owe the operator if the trip were cancelled then (Morten, 29 Oct 2026).
- Cancellation: the client always pays the higher of Archē's scale (A9) and the operator's own charge plus Archē's fee; each confirmation prints the charges for that booking (Morten, 29 Oct 2026).
- Insurance: Archē carries no liability insurance for its first months; all aircraft, passenger and third-party insurance is the operator's. Every trip needs the operator's certificate, at least USD 50M (jet) or 25M (turboprop), valid on every flight date, the aircraft on it, the route inside its cover, confirmed with the insurer or its broker (Morten, 29 Oct 2026).
- Operator library: each operator's terms, insurance and trip figures (no client personal data, no bulk copy of Avinode data) are saved for Archē's own know-how and offered to prefill the next trip (Morten, 29 Oct 2026).
- Clients pay only through a secure payment request from Archē's payment provider (provider still to be agreed). Paynode is removed everywhere and is not named in any text, page, course or document until Morten agrees otherwise (Morten, 26 Oct 2026). Never print, store or send bank details anywhere.

## Bookings
- Simplicity first (Morten, 29 Oct 2026): "We need simplicity and fast flexible bookings, we cannot lose to complex portals." Every step is asked once, by the person who has the information; no double tasks or overlapping procedures; the portal fills in and checks what it can.
- Background checks (Morten, 31 Oct 2026): anything the portal can check against a source (official registry or list, data file, API, or AI web research) is checked in the background while the person works, as part of that step, never as a separate manual task; a discrepancy is flagged on the item itself, with the source and the date checked. The same applies across the whole portal. Efficient, flexible and fast; less is more as long as every aspect is covered. Every paid service or AI call must justify its cost: free official sources first, spend capped and shown, and dropped if it does not pay for itself.
- The Director (Morten, 8 Nov 2026: "lets call it the Director, not owner anywhere as it is just confusing. so owner is the director until a new Director is assigned"): every role, screen and document says "the Director", never "the owner". Until a Director is appointed, the owner is the Director. "Owner" stays only where it means ownership (aircraft owners, beneficial owners, shareholders and dividends) and in code names.
- The broker owns the trip (Morten, 8 Nov 2026): from the first enquiry to the last landing and the final follow-up with the client. Nobody else takes it over; the Director, or a person the Director has mandated, only does the four-eyes steps.
- The Director approves every booking before a confirmation is issued (switch in Today; on by default), or a person the Director has mandated. A price change after approval sends it back.
- Four-eyes rule: nobody prices, approves, issues or records payment on their own trip, the Director and owners included (Morten, 31 Oct 2026: "As there are two owners, even owners if they act as brokers needs 4 eyes"). Another person the Director has mandated ("May authorise" in Team), or the other owner, does it.
- Owners who sell as brokers get the same package as any other broker: the same commission on their own trips, the same terms (Morten, 31 Oct 2026).
- Brokers can set only enquiry, quoted, sent for authorisation or cancelled. Money stages are recorded by the Director, or a person the Director has mandated.
- Brokers and the Director only, until Morten says otherwise (Morten, 7 Nov 2026: "in arche, brokers do it all"; "there are not operations, there are only brokers and owner"): no charter consultants or operations desk. Brokers do every step, including KYC, sanctions screening and the payment request; "Send for authorisation" goes to the Director (the default) or a person the Director has mandated. Navoryx is an operator like any other, with zero links to Archē until Morten says so (the Director-only investor-relations area is the exception).
- KYC / KYB (Morten, 31 Oct 2026): a person who signs and pays gets KYC; a company gets KYB plus KYC on its owners of 25% or more, its controllers and its signatory; a company paying for a person's trip gets KYB as payer and the travellers get name, passport and sanctions check. Operators always KYB (AOC holder, owners and control, sanctions, insurance, audit); brokers KYC (light KYB if they invoice through a company). Identity checked once, refreshed every 12 months or at once on any change (new payer, country, name or ownership, sanctions hit); sanctions screening on every trip for client, payer and travellers. Source of funds only when a trigger applies (third-party or foreign payer, higher-risk country, unusual value for the client, politically exposed person, amber trip check, cash or split payments), never by default. Suspicious transactions are reported to the STRO (CDSA s.45); terrorism-financing information to the Police (TSOFA). Sent and collected by the portal as a booking step, asked once, reused until the refresh date; no ID or bank numbers stored by Archē.
- Every flight is followed to the last landing, the broker follows up within 24 hours, and the client gets a rating request. A score of 3 or less starts service recovery.
- Client ratings may be quoted publicly only with the client's written consent. Never write, buy or reward reviews.

## Prospects and data
- Organisations first. Named people may be stored in any prospect list only as business contacts: a professional acting in a business role at an organisation, with name, role and work email or phone, only as published by the organisation itself or taken from a licensed professional database (approved by Morten, 20 Oct 2026). Never private individuals as such: no home addresses, personal phones or personal emails, no wealth or family details. No guessed emails or phone numbers, no bought consumer or wealth lists, no LinkedIn log-in or scraping, no scraping of competitor or marketplace boards, no copying Avinode data out of Avinode.
- Every first message to a named contact says where we found their details and offers an opt-out. An opt-out deletes the person's details at once in every list (only a one-way code is kept so they are never added again). Contacts not reached within 12 months are deleted. Brokers see contacts only on their own leads; opening contacts is logged; no export of contact details. Contacts in the investor list stay Director-only. Navoryx investor relations run from this list (Morten, 5 Nov 2026: "the portal as the engine to control investor relations and also the database for potential investors"): access requests from the Navoryx investor page are filed here with their sanctions result (`/api/ir`, `api/_nx/ir.mjs`, module `src-modules/irelations.js`); only the Director gives access, and invitations and approval emails go out only when the Director presses Send. Archē is never named on the Navoryx website or in its emails, and the shared audit log gets no investor names or addresses. Both sides follow `docs/IR-PORTAL-CONTRACT.md`, kept identical in navoryx-website and arche-platform: any change to it is made in both repositories in the same piece of work, with both test suites run (Morten, 5 Nov 2026: both flows always aligned, so either side can fix issues).
- Suppliers (Morten, 9 Nov 2026): service partners sit in their own sections and are never mixed: aviation services (FBOs and business-aviation handlers, trip support, in-flight catering), hotels (crew hotels within 15 minutes with their own shuttle; hotels and resorts of five-star or luxury standard for our clients), ground transport, helicopters (operator certificate required) and yachts, building towards full-service bookings; one record per company with all its airports. Operators come from the audit registries (ARGUS, WYVERN) and the authorities' official certificate lists, read without AI, with clear filters, each marked with what to confirm before the first booking ("1 yes, but with clear filtering options for our purposes").
- Real client and broker data never goes into this repository. Tests use example data only.
- Any outbound message or action that cannot be undone needs a person to confirm it. Do not connect an e-signature service unless Morten asks.

## Writing
- Plain, short English. Say "Archē Executive" in branded text; the legal name is Arche Executive Pte. Ltd.
- Archē is a Singapore company and stays Singaporean until the Bangkok office opens: no Bangkok office, Bangkok address or Thai phone number on the website, in signatures, offers, agreements or client documents (Morten, 26 Oct 2026). Thai routes and airports are fine. The website switch is `BANGKOK_OFFICE` in `apps/website/build.py`.
- The Word manual is the master. Change the manual first, then rebuild the academies from it.
- The external academy must contain nothing Archē-internal; `apps/academy/build/ext.js` fails the build if it does.
- Notices to members (`apps/portal/src-modules/notices.js`): ask Morten first whether a change needs a notice at all, and agree the wording, before adding one. A notice in the code is only a draft; nothing reaches members until Morten publishes it in Team (Morten, 26 Oct 2026).

## Website content (Morten, 27 Oct 2026)
- We are a broker, not an airline: pages are about places, airports and needs, never "routes we fly". Every page ends with "any aircraft, any route, upon availability".
- Only verified, correct facts, each with its source and the date it was checked. No guesses. Better fewer pages and correct than many and wrong. The build refuses a fact without a source or date.
- No prices until Archē has its own quote data; then only ranges with the basis and date.
- Sanctions (Morten, 27 Oct 2026): sanctioned countries, territories, companies and people are avoided in KYC (manual Module 3.2, including ownership and control) and are never listed, named, offered or promoted on the website; this includes Russia, Iran, Iraq, Syria and Venezuela. The list of places is `apps/website/content/excluded.json`; the content build, `check_site.py` and the quote form's airport list all use it. Sanctions are covered only in general words in the terms of business (charter confirmation Part B, clause B6), which name the regimes (UN, Singapore, US, EU, UK) but list no countries. The website names no companies or people other than authorities, airport operators and the sources it cites.
- Keep the site's layout, design and watermarks on every new page; speed and structure come first (static pages, no extra scripts).
- A saved copy of the site before this work: branch `saved/website-2026-10-27` (and the zip in Final versions).

## All or none (Morten, 8 Nov 2026)
- "Each change we do, also change all related documents and manuals. We cannot have a situation where only half is revised, all or none, and all means audited."
- A change is done only when every related document, manual, academy, portal text, agreement, client document and test says the same thing, in the order of `docs/CHANGE-MAP.md` (master first). If one part cannot be changed yet, change nothing and ask Morten.
- Audited means: `python3 scripts/check_all_revised.py` passes (also run in the Portal checks), the tests for every part touched pass, a repository-wide search finds none of the old wording, and the report says what changed where and what was run.

## Full audit (Morten, 8 Nov 2026, locked)

Nothing is reported as audited until every item on the list is done; anything not checked is named as not checked.

Update `docs/ARCHE-HANDOVER.md` (the file a new chat starts from) and the copy in the claude.ai project at `arche/arche-handover.md` at the end of each working session.
