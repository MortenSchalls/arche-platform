Independent Broker Consultancy Agreement

Arche Executive Pte. Ltd. · Signing version, 24 October 2026

1. Parties and definitions

This agreement is made on the date of the last signature in Schedule 3 between:

  1. Arche Executive Pte. Ltd., a company incorporated in Singapore (UEN [●]), whose registered office is at [●], Singapore, trading as “Archē Executive” (Archē); and
  2. the person or company named in Schedule 2 (the Consultant).

Background. Archē arranges private jet charter flights for clients as their broker, using licensed aircraft operators. Archē wishes to engage the Consultant, on a commission-only and independent basis, to find and look after clients, and the Consultant wishes to provide those services from outside Singapore and Thailand.

Definitions. In this agreement:

  • Booking means one charter transaction for a client under a charter agreement that Archē has approved and issued, covering every flight in it, whether a single flight, a multi-leg trip or a long-term charter, together with all the work in clause 3.1 from first enquiry to final settlement.
  • Completed Booking means a Booking in which every flight has been operated and landed, the client has paid in full and the payment has cleared in Archē’s account, all extras and items at cost have been invoiced and settled, the operator and suppliers have been paid, and the Consultant has finished the after-flight follow-up and recorded everything in the Portal.
  • Client Price means the total price the client pays Archē for a Booking, excluding taxes, items invoiced at cost and refunds.
  • Gross Profit means the Client Price less the amount Archē pays the aircraft operator and the direct costs of the trip.
  • Standard Booking means a Booking value of USD 45,000 of Client Price; Bookings are counted by value as set out in Schedule 1.
  • Portal means Archē’s online broker portal, or any system that replaces it.
  • Registered Client means a client or prospect recorded in the Portal as the Consultant’s lead, in line with clause 7.3.
  • Operating Rules means the Archē Broker Academy, the Portal guide and the written rules Archē issues for brokers, as updated from time to time.
  • Territory means the countries in which the Consultant lives and works, as stated in Schedule 2.

2. Appointment and independent status

2.1 Appointment. Archē engages the Consultant on a non-exclusive basis to introduce clients to Archē and to support those clients through their Bookings, on the terms of this agreement.

2.2 Independent contractor. The Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee, worker, partner or joint venturer of Archē. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship. The Consultant decides their own working hours, methods and place of work (within clause 2.5), provides their own equipment, phone and internet, and bears their own business costs.

2.3 No authority to bind. The Consultant has no authority to conclude, sign, amend or cancel any contract on Archē’s behalf, or to accept any order, set or agree any price, grant any discount, or make any commitment that binds Archē. Every quote, charter agreement, confirmation, invoice and payment request is issued by Archē through the Portal after Archē’s own review and approval. The Consultant must not hold themselves out as having such authority.

2.4 How the Consultant presents themselves. The Consultant may use the title “Independent Broker, Archē Executive” and an Archē email address provided for that purpose, and must make clear when asked that they act as an independent consultant to Archē. The Consultant must never present themselves or Archē as an aircraft operator.

2.5 Place of work. The Consultant provides the services from the Territory only. The Consultant will not perform the services while physically in Singapore or Thailand, other than for short business visits agreed in writing in advance, and will tell Archē at least 30 days before moving their residence to another country.

2.6 Other work. The Consultant may carry on other business, provided that during this agreement they do not arrange private aviation charter for, or introduce charter clients to, any other broker, operator or platform without Archē’s written consent.

2.7 Consultant’s promises. The Consultant confirms that they are free to enter into this agreement, that doing so breaks no other contract or restriction binding on them, and that the details in Schedule 2 are true.

3. Services and how we work

3.1 Responsibility for each Booking. The Consultant is responsible for each of their Bookings from first enquiry to completion, including every leg of a multi-leg trip and the whole period of a long-term charter. For each Booking the Consultant does everything the Booking needs, including but not limited to the following:

  1. finds the client and takes the client’s full trip requirements;
  2. sources suitable aircraft and operator options through the Portal, for Archē’s review and approval;
  3. creates the booking in the Portal and keeps it fully updated at every stage;
  4. takes and arranges the client’s special requests, such as catering, pets, special equipment or onboard needs;
  5. makes sure ground handling is in place at every airport on the trip;
  6. arranges the client’s transport to and from the airport, and access to the terminal or aircraft;
  7. gathers the passenger, crew-related and client documents the chartered operator requires (such as passports, visas and passenger details) and sends them to the operator on time;
  8. follows each flight from departure to the last landing and stays reachable for the client and Archē’s operations team while the trip runs, dealing with delays and changes as they arise;
  9. completes the follow-up with the client within 24 hours of the last landing and the client’s rating request; and
  10. makes sure all extras are recorded and invoiced, and the Booking is closed in the Portal.

All arrangements with operators, handlers and other suppliers are made in Archē’s name through the Portal, within clause 2.3.

3.2 Portal only. Every enquiry, quote, booking, document and payment for a client introduced under this agreement must go through the Portal. The Consultant must not arrange, quote or take a booking outside the Portal, or move a client’s business to any other party.

3.3 Archē’s decisions. Archē decides in its sole discretion whether to accept a client or a trip, which operator to use, the Client Price and the terms. Archē may decline any trip, including for safety, compliance or credit reasons, and no commission is due on a trip Archē declines.

3.4 Client money. Clients pay Archē directly through Archē’s payment request. The Consultant must never receive, hold or forward client money, and must never give a client any bank details.

3.5 Status changes. In the Portal the Consultant may move a trip only to the stages enquiry, quoted, submitted or cancelled. Stages involving money and confirmation are set by Archē’s operations team.

3.6 Training and standards. Before handling a first trip, the Consultant completes the Archē Broker Academy. The Consultant follows the Operating Rules, which Archē may update by notice in the Portal or by email. A change to the Operating Rules does not change the commission in Schedule 1.

3.7 Tools. Archē gives the Consultant a personal Portal login and a dedicated Archē email address. The Consultant uses only that address, with the signature and disclaimer in Schedule 5, for all email about Archē business, and never a personal or other business address. For messaging, the Consultant uses only the Archē company accounts on WhatsApp, LINE, WeChat and any other channel Archē provides for clients, and the dedicated WhatsApp groups Archē creates for internal communication. All these accounts and groups belong to Archē, and the Consultant’s access ends with this agreement. The login is personal and must not be shared. Access ends when this agreement ends.

3.8 Standards of conduct. Archē serves the most demanding private clients, and every contact with a client, operator or supplier must meet that standard. The Consultant always acts in Archē’s name and in its interest (within clause 2.3), and follows the Standards of Conduct in Schedule 4 at all times.

3.9 Enquiries received by Archē. Enquiries that reach Archē directly, through its website, its charter email or any other channel, go first to Archē, which decides whether to release them. Released enquiries are offered to all brokers in the Portal on a first-come, first-served basis: the first broker to take an enquiry in the Portal receives it, and the client becomes that broker’s Registered Client. Archē may instead assign any enquiry, or reassign it at any time, to the broker it considers most suitable. A broker who takes or is given an enquiry contacts the client within 15 minutes.

3.10 Booking cycle target. The Consultant aims to complete each Booking from first enquiry to signed confirmation and the client’s payment within 5 days, or within any other target Archē sets in the Portal from time to time. Archē may reassign an enquiry or Booking that is not progressing, and clause 4.5 then applies to any commission.

4. Commission, payment and taxes

4.1 Commission. Archē pays the Consultant commission on the Gross Profit of each Booking for a Registered Client, at the rates in Schedule 1. Commission is the Consultant’s only payment under this agreement. There is no salary, retainer, draw or minimum.

4.2 When commission is earned. Commission is calculated and earned only on a Completed Booking, once the whole transaction is finalised. No commission is earned on a Booking, or any flight or leg in it, before then, including for multi-leg trips and long-term charters. A Completed Booking counts in the calendar month in which it becomes complete.

4.3 Statement and payment. By the 15th of each month, Archē issues a statement showing each Booking earned in the previous month, its Client Price, Gross Profit and the commission. Archē pays the commission in USD by the same date, by bank transfer to an account in the Consultant’s own name that the Consultant registers with Archē’s payment provider. The statement serves as the Consultant’s invoice unless the Consultant’s local law requires them to issue their own.

4.4 Refunds and chargebacks. Commission is paid only on completed, fully paid Bookings, so a deduction is rare. It applies only if, within 180 days after the last flight of a Booking on which commission was paid, (a) a card payment is reversed (chargeback) or a payment is reclaimed by a bank or an authority, or (b) Archē refunds part of the price to the client for a service failure. Archē then deducts the commission on the amount lost from the Consultant’s next commission payments. Archē never asks the Consultant to pay money back, and makes no deduction where the loss was caused by Archē or the operator and not by the Consultant. Archē shows the Consultant the Portal records for the Booking concerned.

4.5 Shared bookings. If two brokers worked on one Booking, Archē decides the split fairly based on the Portal record, and the total commission does not exceed one broker’s commission.

4.6 Disputes about a statement. The Consultant raises any query within 30 days of a statement. Archē will show the Consultant the Portal records for the Bookings concerned.

4.7 Expenses. Each party bears its own costs. Archē reimburses an expense only if it approved it in writing in advance.

4.8 Taxes. The Consultant is solely responsible for their own income tax, social security, registration and any sales tax or VAT on their commission in the Territory, and indemnifies Archē against any such amount claimed from Archē. Commission is stated exclusive of any VAT or similar tax the Consultant must charge. If Archē is required by law to withhold tax from a payment, it will pay the net amount and give the Consultant evidence of the tax paid.

4.9 Work done in Singapore. If any part of the services is performed in Singapore, the Consultant tells Archē in advance, and Archē may withhold from the commission for that part any Singapore tax it is required to withhold.

5. Compliance

5.1 Laws. The Consultant complies with all laws that apply to their work, including the laws of the Territory and of Singapore, and holds any registration or licence the Territory requires for their business.

5.2 Anti-bribery. The Consultant must not offer, give, ask for or accept any bribe, kickback or improper payment or benefit, in money or otherwise, in connection with Archē’s business, and complies with Singapore’s Prevention of Corruption Act 1960 and the anti-bribery law of the Territory. This includes payments to client staff, operator staff or public officials. The Consultant tells Archē at once of any such request.

5.3 Sanctions and checks. The Consultant must not introduce, or deal on Archē’s behalf with, any person or company on a sanctions list of the United Nations, United States, European Union or United Kingdom, or any trip to a sanctioned destination. The Consultant gives Archē the client information it asks for to complete its checks, and tells Archē of anything that suggests money laundering, sanctions evasion or unlawful carriage.

5.4 Aviation. Flights are operated by licensed operators chosen by Archē. The Consultant makes no statement about an aircraft, operator, safety standard or flight time that Archē has not confirmed in writing, and passes any client safety concern to Archē at once.

5.5 Data protection. The Consultant handles personal data only for this agreement and in line with Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012, the data protection law of the Territory and the Operating Rules. In particular the Consultant:

  1. keeps client and contact data in the Portal only and does not export, copy or keep it elsewhere;
  2. records named prospect contacts only as business contacts acting for an organisation, using details the organisation publishes itself or a licensed database Archē approves, and never guessed emails or phone numbers, personal addresses or wealth or family details;
  3. does not scrape websites, LinkedIn or marketplace boards, and does not copy data out of Avinode or similar systems;
  4. says in each first message where the contact’s details were found and offers an opt-out, and records any opt-out in the Portal at once; and
  5. tells Archē within 24 hours of any loss or misuse of personal data.

5.6 Reviews and marketing. The Consultant must never write, buy, reward or ask for false reviews, and may quote a client publicly only with the client’s written consent obtained through Archē. Marketing that uses the Archē name or logo must follow Archē’s brand rules and be approved by Archē in advance.

5.7 Personal data sent outside Singapore. Archē makes client and prospect personal data available to the Consultant only in the Territory, through the Portal. For that data the Consultant must, to a standard at least comparable to Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012:

  1. use and disclose it only to perform this agreement, and not pass it to anyone else without Archē’s written consent;
  2. keep it accurate and complete through the Portal;
  3. protect it with reasonable security, including a secured device, a strong password and no shared logins;
  4. keep it no longer than needed, and delete any copy when this agreement ends;
  5. follow Archē’s data protection policy as part of the Operating Rules; and
  6. pass any request from a person to access or correct their data to Archē within 2 business days, and help Archē answer it.

The Consultant may not access the data from any country other than the Territory without Archē’s written consent.

6. Confidentiality and intellectual property

6.1 Confidentiality. The Consultant keeps confidential, during and after this agreement, all information about Archē’s clients, prospects, operators, prices, margins, Portal, methods and finances, and uses it only to perform this agreement. This does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the Consultant, or that the law requires to be disclosed (after telling Archē where allowed).

6.2 Intellectual property. The Portal, the Archē Broker Academy, the Archē name and logo, and all material Archē provides remain Archē’s property. Anything the Consultant creates for Archē’s business under this agreement, including client records and marketing material, belongs to Archē, and the Consultant assigns it to Archē on creation.

7. Clients and non-solicitation

7.1 Clients are Archē’s. Clients and prospects introduced under this agreement are clients of Archē. The Consultant’s reward for introducing and looking after them is the commission in Schedule 1.

7.2 Repeat bookings. While this agreement continues, the Consultant earns commission on every Booking for a Registered Client, including repeat bookings.

7.3 Registration of leads. A client or prospect becomes a Registered Client of the Consultant when the Consultant first records it in the Portal, unless it is already registered to another broker or to Archē. A registration lapses if no Booking is made for that client within 12 months of the last Booking or, for a prospect, within 12 months of registration. Archē decides any conflict between brokers based on the Portal record.

7.4 Non-solicitation. During this agreement and for 12 months after it ends, the Consultant must not, for themselves or for any other person, solicit or accept private aviation charter business from any client the Consultant dealt with under this agreement in the last 12 months before it ended, or encourage any Archē broker, staff member or operator partner to end or reduce their work with Archē.

8. Term, termination and liability

8.1 Term. This agreement starts on the start date in Schedule 2 and continues until ended under this clause.

8.2 Ending on notice. Either party may end this agreement for any reason by giving the other 30 days’ written notice.

8.3 Ending at once. Archē may end this agreement at once by written notice if the Consultant breaches clause 2.3, 3.2, 3.4, 5 or 6, commits any other material breach not put right within 14 days of notice, acts dishonestly, brings Archē into disrepute, or seriously or repeatedly falls short of the Standards of Conduct in Schedule 4, including through poor client feedback as set out in that schedule.

8.4 Commission after the end. After this agreement ends, Archē pays commission on Bookings that were approved before the end date and become Completed Bookings within 6 months after it. Where Archē or another broker has to finish the work in clause 3.1 on such a Booking, Archē reduces the commission fairly to reflect that work. No commission is due on any other Booking. If the agreement ended under clause 8.3, Archē may withhold commission on Bookings affected by the breach.

8.5 On ending. Portal access ends on the end date. The Consultant returns or deletes all Archē information and client data and confirms this in writing within 7 days. Clauses 4.4, 4.8, 5.5, 6, 7.4, 8 and 9 continue after the end.

8.6 No compensation. Except for commission due under clause 8.4, no compensation, indemnity or goodwill payment is due to the Consultant when this agreement ends, to the extent the applicable law allows.

8.7 Liability. Neither party is liable to the other for loss of profit or indirect loss. Archē’s total liability under this agreement is limited to the commission due to the Consultant under it. Nothing limits liability for fraud or for anything the law does not allow to be limited.

8.8 Indemnity. The Consultant indemnifies Archē against loss, fines and reasonable legal costs arising from the Consultant’s breach of clause 2.3, 3.2, 3.4, 4.8, 5 or 6.

8.9 Insurance. Archē is arranging insurance for its charter brokerage business and will confirm to the Consultant in writing when it is in place. The Consultant arranges any insurance their own business or local law requires.

9. General

9.1 Whole agreement. This agreement and its schedules are the whole agreement between the parties about its subject and replace any earlier offer or discussion.

9.2 Changes. A change to this agreement or to Schedule 1 is valid only in writing signed by both parties. Archē may update the Operating Rules under clause 3.6.

9.3 Assignment. The Consultant may not assign or subcontract this agreement. If the Consultant is a company, the services are performed personally by the individual named in Schedule 2. Archē may assign this agreement to a company in its group or to a buyer of its business, on notice.

9.4 Notices. Notices are given in writing by email to the addresses in Schedule 2 (for Archē, the address stated there), and take effect on the next business day in Singapore.

9.5 Third parties. No person other than the parties has any right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 to enforce any term of this agreement.

9.6 Severability. If any term is invalid or unenforceable in any place, it is limited to the extent needed there and the rest of the agreement continues.

9.7 Counterparts and signature. This agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original.

9.8 Language. This agreement is in English. Any translation is for convenience only.

9.9 Governing law. This agreement and any dispute arising from it are governed by the laws of Singapore.

9.10 Disputes. The parties first try to settle any dispute by discussion between the Consultant and an Archē director for 30 days. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this agreement, including any question about its existence, validity or termination, that is not settled in that time shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) under the Arbitration Rules of the SIAC for the time being in force. The seat of arbitration is Singapore, the tribunal consists of one arbitrator, and the language is English. Either party may still seek urgent interim relief from any competent court.

Schedule 1: Commission

Commission is a share of the Gross Profit on the Consultant’s Bookings, with a higher rate once the Consultant passes 8 Standard Bookings in a calendar month.

Standard Bookings in the calendar monthShare of Gross Profit
1 to 827%
9 and every one after35%
  • Bookings are counted by Client Price: USD 45,000 of Client Price is one Standard Booking. A smaller Booking counts as part of one and a larger Booking as more than one. The 35% rate applies to the Gross Profit above the first 8 Standard Bookings in the month.
  • The count starts again on the first day of each calendar month.
  • There is no cap on commission.
  • Example: a USD 45,000 Booking with USD 4,050 Gross Profit earns USD 1,093.50 at 27%, or USD 1,417.50 at 35%.

Supervision share. While the Consultant is supervised under the Operating Rules and Archē has named a supervisor for them, 3 percentage points of the Gross Profit on each Booking approved in that time go to the supervisor instead of the Consultant (24% instead of 27%, or 32% instead of 35%; USD 121.50 on the example above). This continues until Archē ends the Consultant’s supervision. Where an owner of Archē is the supervisor, those 3 points stay with Archē.

A Consultant whom Archē names as supervisor earns 3 percentage points of the Gross Profit on each such Booking of the person they supervise, on the same terms as their own commission (clauses 4.2 to 4.4). If Archē finds that the supervisor approved a step on that Booking that broke the Operating Rules, the share on that Booking stays with Archē.

Schedule 4: Standards of Conduct

We serve the top 0.1% of travellers, and we deliver that standard every time. These rules apply to every contact the Consultant has for Archē: calls, messages, emails, online and in-person meetings.

1. Act for Archē

  • Present yourself as Archē Executive, never under your own brand or another company’s.
  • Email clients, operators and suppliers only from your dedicated Archē address, with the Schedule 5 signature and disclaimer unchanged. Never use a personal email address for Archē business, and never send bulk or marketing emails without Archē’s approval. Message clients only through the Archē company accounts on WhatsApp, LINE and WeChat, never from a personal account or number, and talk with the Archē team only in the dedicated WhatsApp groups Archē creates.
  • Put Archē’s reputation and the client’s interest ahead of your own commission.

2. Client first

  • The client’s safety, time and comfort come first in every decision.
  • Reply to a client within 1 hour during their working day, and within 15 minutes while their trip is under way.
  • Anticipate needs before the client asks, and confirm every detail in writing.
  • Never promise anything Archē has not confirmed. If something goes wrong, tell the client early and honestly, with a solution.

3. How we speak and write

  • Always polite, calm and warm. Address clients formally until they invite otherwise.
  • No slang, jokes at anyone’s expense or emoji in client messages.
  • Never argue with a client, and never criticise a competitor, an operator or a colleague.
  • Short, clear, correct English, or the client’s own language where you are fluent.

4. Online meetings

  • Camera on, using the Archē meeting background provided.
  • Business attire: a dark suit or jacket with a collared shirt or blouse, or equivalent formal business dress.
  • A quiet, well-lit place, a good headset and a stable connection; join a few minutes early.
  • Display name: First name Last name · Archē Executive. No eating, smoking or other tasks during the meeting.
  • Host client meetings only on the Archē company Teams account. Never host them on a personal Zoom, Google Meet or other account; joining a link that a client or operator sends is fine.
  • Never record a meeting without the client’s clear consent.

5. Discretion

  • Never disclose who our clients are, where they fly or with whom, to anyone outside Archē.
  • Never post about clients, flights or aircraft on social media, and never take photos of clients.
  • Never use a client relationship for your own benefit.

6. Personal conduct

  • Never deal with a client or a live trip under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Do not accept a gift or hospitality worth more than USD 100 without Archē’s written approval.

7. Feedback and consequences

  • Every client is asked to rate their trip. A rating of 3 or less out of 5, or a complaint, starts service recovery led by Archē and a review with the Consultant.
  • A first shortfall leads to a written warning and coaching.
  • Two ratings of 3 or less within 6 months, one serious complaint that Archē upholds, or any breach of the discretion rules may lead Archē to end this agreement at once under clause 8.3.
