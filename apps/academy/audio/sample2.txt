Let's talk about time. In aviation, everything runs on U-T-C. You'll also hear it called Zulu. It's the same time everywhere in the world. Your clients, of course, think in their own local time.

So every quote and every trip brief you send shows local times, and says so clearly. And on long flights, check the arrival date. Crossing time zones, or the date line, can move it to the next day.

One more thing. When a client says "tomorrow morning", don't assume. Confirm the date, the time and the time zone, in writing.

Now, try this one. A client in Bangkok wants to leave at nine in the morning, local time. The operator's schedule shows oh-two-hundred Zulu. Do they match?
