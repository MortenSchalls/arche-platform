Module zero, screen five. Time.

Aviation works in U T C, also called Zulu: the same time everywhere. Clients think in local time.

Every quote and trip brief shows local times, and says so. Check arrival dates on long flights: crossing time zones, or the date line, can change the arrival date.

When a client says "tomorrow morning", confirm the date, the time and the time zone, in writing.

Try it. A client in Bangkok wants to leave at nine a m, local time. The operator's schedule shows zero two hundred Zulu. Do they match?
