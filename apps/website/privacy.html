Who we are

The company responsible for the personal data described here is Arche Executive Pte. Ltd., trading as Archē Executive ("we"), a Singapore company. We follow Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). Archē Executive arranges private jet charters as broker for our clients; licensed operators fly every trip.

Our Data Protection Officer answers questions, requests and complaints about personal data at [privacy contact email], during Singapore business hours.

What this notice covers

This website and its quote, call-back and empty-leg alert forms; messages you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp; the trips you book with us; the business contacts we write to at organisations; and the brokers who apply to work with us or work with us, including the identity checks we make on them.

What we collect

  • From the quote form: your route, dates, number of passengers, your name and how to reach you (phone, WhatsApp or email), and, if you answer it, whether you have flown privately before.
  • From the call-back form: your phone number.
  • From the empty-leg alerts sign-up: your email and the routes you fly. If you tick the box on the quote form, we also send you empty-leg alerts and occasional news; this box is never ticked for you.
  • When you book: the details the operator and the authorities need for the flight, such as passenger names and travel documents, and the identity and sanctions checks the law asks of us for the person who signs, the person or company that pays and those travelling. We ask for these only when you confirm a trip, and we do not keep passport, ID or bank numbers ourselves.
  • Business contacts: the name, role and work email or phone of a professional at an organisation, only as published by the organisation itself or taken from a licensed professional database. Never home addresses, personal phones or personal emails.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, not even your language choice. It uses no advertising, no tracking pixels and no outside analytics services. The provider that hosts the website processes network addresses briefly to deliver pages and to protect the website from misuse.

Website statistics

To improve the website and to follow up interest from organisations, our own website records each visit without cookies and without storing anything in your browser: the page, the website or campaign you came from, your country and city (worked out from your network address, which we do not store), the type of device, the sections you reached and what you used, such as the quote form, call, WhatsApp or email. We cannot tell who you are from these records.

Links we send to an organisation can carry a short code. If you open such a link, we know that the link sent to your organisation was opened, and what was used on the site. We use this only to follow up the conversation we started, never to build a profile of a person.

If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, nothing is recorded. Statistics are kept for 13 months and seen only by the Archē Executive team.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request and prepare quotes.
  • To arrange and look after your flight once you book, and to ask how it went.
  • To meet our legal duties: identity, anti-money-laundering and sanctions checks, the records the law asks us to keep, and reports the law requires to the authorities.
  • To send empty-leg alerts and news, only if you asked for them.
  • To introduce our services to organisations, through their business contacts.
  • To keep the website and our records secure.

We use personal data only for these purposes. We never sell, rent or trade it.

Your consent

You give consent by ticking the box on each form before sending it; nothing is sent without it. Alerts and news need their own separate tick, never ticked for you. When you write to us, you agree that we use your details to answer you. You may withdraw your consent at any time; we then stop, unless the law requires us to keep a record.

For visitors in the European Union, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Thailand, the legal bases are: steps you ask us to take before a contract and carrying out the contract (quotes and bookings); our legal duties (checks and records); our legitimate interest in running and improving the website, in keeping it secure and in writing to business contacts about our services; and your consent (alerts and news).

Messages from us

We write to you only about what you asked for. Alerts and news go only to people who asked for them, and every message tells you how to stop further messages. We do not call or message a Singapore number listed on the Do Not Call Registry for marketing.

The first message we send to a business contact says where we found their details and how to stop further messages. If you ask us to stop, we delete your details at once from every list, keeping only a one-way code so that you are never added again.

Who we share it with

Only the Archē Executive team handling your request sees it. When you book, we share what is needed with the aircraft operator, ground handlers and authorities for that flight, with the service that carries out identity and sanctions checks, and with our payment provider. Our website, database and email, and the AI tools our team uses to prepare and check trips, are run by service providers that process data only on our instructions and are bound to protect it. Our team does not enter passport, ID or bank numbers into AI tools, and under its terms our AI provider may not use your data to train its models. Our professional advisers, who owe us confidentiality, and authorities where the law requires it may also receive it.

Where it is kept

Our service providers may store or process data outside the country you are in, including in Singapore, Australia and the United States. Whenever personal data leaves Singapore, we make sure the recipient is bound by contract to protect it to a standard comparable to the PDPA; for visitors in the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, by the standard contractual clauses approved for such transfers.

How long we keep it

Requests that do not lead to a booking: twelve months after our last contact with you. Booking records and the checks made for them: as long as the law requires us to keep them. Business contacts: deleted at once when they ask us to stop, and after twelve months if we have not been in touch. Alerts and news: until you unsubscribe. Website statistics: 13 months.

How we protect it

Everything is sent over encrypted connections. Access is limited to the people who need it, every team member signs in, and backups are encrypted. Opening a business contact's details is logged.

If a data breach is likely to cause you significant harm, we will tell you, and we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission as the PDPA requires.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you and how it has been used or disclosed in the past year, ask us to correct it, withdraw your consent or ask us to delete it. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer answer a request or arrange a trip. Write to [privacy contact email]. We answer within thirty days, or tell you within that time when we will, and we make no charge.

Visitors in the European Union, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Thailand may also ask us to restrict or stop using their data and to receive it in a portable form, and may complain to their data protection authority, such as Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee or the authority in their EU or UK country. In Singapore, if we cannot resolve a concern, you may contact the Personal Data Protection Commission.

Children

This website is not intended for anyone under eighteen, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. We arrange travel for children only with a parent or guardian who books for them.
