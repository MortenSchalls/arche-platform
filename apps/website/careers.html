Build your own charter business with us

Archē Executive is a private jet charter broker based in Singapore. We are inviting a small group of independent brokers to sell private charter with us, for clients in Asia and worldwide.

What you get

  • You keep up to 35% of the Archē fee on every trip you sell, with no cap.
  • You work from where you live, outside Singapore and Thailand, and plan your own days. There are no sales targets; clients expect a quick answer, and the portal helps you give it.
  • Our broker portal does the checking with you: operators, insurance, identity and sanctions checks, the charter confirmation and the payment request, each with a second pair of eyes before anything is issued.
  • The Archē Broker Academy teaches the work step by step, at your own pace, before your first client.

Who we are looking for

People who know clients who fly privately, or the people who arrange it for them: from private aviation, luxury travel, hospitality, yachting, wealth management or corporate travel. You write clear English, you answer quickly and you take rules seriously. You do not need to have sold a charter before; the academy shows you how.

How it works

You join as an independent consultant, not as an employee, working from outside Singapore and Thailand. You run your own business and hold your own business, tax and social security registrations where you live. Commission is earned on completed bookings: flown, paid by the client and paid to the operator.

The steps: you write to us, we invite you to the portal, you read our offer and take the academy, we meet for an assessment, and you sign the agreement in the portal.

How to apply

Write to careers@archeaviation.com with a few lines about your background and the market you know, and your CV if you have one. We answer every application.

Fair selection

We choose on experience and ability alone, whatever your age, gender, race, religion, nationality, family situation or disability.

Your details

We use what you send only to consider your application. If we do not go ahead together, we delete it twelve months after our last contact, or sooner if you ask. Our Privacy notice explains the rest.

Any aircraft, any route, upon availability.
