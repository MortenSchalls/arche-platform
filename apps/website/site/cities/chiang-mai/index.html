Private flights to and from Chiang Mai

Chiang Mai International Airport is open around the clock, with customs on site. A private flight needs a Thai permit and a slot, and this season the airport limits night flights and ground time. What the official sources say.

Chiang Mai International Airport

The airport is run by Airports of Thailand Public Company Limited (AOT)1 and lies 4 km south-west of the city1. It is open 24 hours1, with customs and immigration open 24 hours1, so an international arrival can be planned at any hour the night limits allow. It has one runway, 18/36, 3,400 m long and 45 m wide (3,100 m available for landing on runway 18)1.

Parking is set by stand size: largest stands (bays 3-8) take up to code E aircraft; bays 9, 12, 15, 19, 20L, 20R up to code C2. One ground rule to know: aircraft may taxi out of a stand under own power only if code A or B (wingspan up to 24 m); power-back is not permitted1.

Permit and time slot

Thailand asks for prior approval from CAAT for every foreign non-scheduled flight, applied for at least 3 business days ahead through the FPOS system; this also covers non-commercial (private) flights3. For Chiang Mai itself: Level 3 (coordinated) airport: slots are coordinated by CAAT and non-scheduled flights must obtain a slot (business aviation applies with a General Aviation Clearance Request, GCR)3.

The airport also asks for notice: all flights must be coordinated with the airport in advance by the airline or handling agent; non-scheduled flights may stay on the ground no more than 4 hours (Winter 2026/27)2. A trip that keeps the aircraft on the ground for longer is planned with a positioning flight, and we show you that in the quote.

Night flights

The slot coordinator limits night movements to protect the neighbours: Winter 2026/27 (25 Oct 2026 - 27 Mar 2027): only emergency and medical flights 02:00-05:59 local (19:00-22:59 UTC); 00:00-00:59 local (17:00-17:59 UTC) limited to 4 flights (8 movements) a day and 01:00-01:59 local (18:00-18:59 UTC) to 2 flights (4 movements) a day, for noise2. The limits are set season by season, so we check the notice for your dates before we quote.

Flights from elsewhere in Thailand

A flight from Bangkok, Phuket or another Thai airport to Chiang Mai is a domestic flight. In Thailand, charter flights by foreign carriers may not carry passengers between points in Thailand3, so we check that the operator is allowed to fly the leg before you see a quote. Our cabotage guide explains the rule.

Arriving and leaving

Every person travelling needs a valid passport and any visa the country asks for. The operator and its handling agent arrange customs, immigration and ground handling for your arrival and departure, and tell you where to meet the crew.

Your trip brief names the airport, where private flights are handled and the contact on the ground. Your broker stays with you from the first enquiry to the last landing, and follows up after the trip.

Within limits.
