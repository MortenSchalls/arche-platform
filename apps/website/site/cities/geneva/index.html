Private flights to and from Geneva

Geneva Airport runs private flights through its own permission system. What that means for your trip.

Draft · waiting for approval

Permission for every private flight

Geneva Airport is run by Genève Aéroport, an autonomous public institution owned by the State of Geneva1. For private flights the key rule is permission: prior permission (PPR) needed for business and general aviation; bookings open 5 days ahead, made through the handling agent2. The permission number then goes into the flight plan. Business aviation is a large part of the airport's traffic: general and business aviation made up 23% of flights in 20253.

On busy dates permission can be harder to get at short notice. We ask the operator to book as soon as your plans are firm.

Night rules

The airport is open at all times, subject to the night-flight rules4. Under the airport's rules, any planned commercial take-off or landing between 22:00 and 06:00 needs the airport operator's prior approval4. For business aviation, noise quotas apply to the night period 22:00-05:59; for business and general aviation they cover take-offs after 22:00 by aircraft in noise classes IV and V5. A late departure is therefore possible only with the right aircraft and the right approval, and we check both with the operator first.

The aircraft and the operator

Because of the noise rules, the aircraft type matters at Geneva in the evening. We ask the operator for the aircraft's noise class when your times are late, and we tell you before you confirm if the flight would not be allowed.

Questions we are asked

How far ahead must a private flight to Geneva be booked?

The airport's permission system opens shortly before the flight: prior permission (PPR) needed for business and general aviation; bookings open 5 days ahead, made through the handling agent2. We arrange it with the operator as soon as your plans are firm.

Does Archē Executive operate the flight?

No. We are a broker. A licensed operator flies every trip, and it is named in your quote and charter agreement before you confirm.
