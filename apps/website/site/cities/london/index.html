Private flights to and from London

London has several airports used by private jets, and they differ most in their opening hours. How the main ones compare, from the airports' own published rules.

Farnborough

Farnborough, south-west of London, describes itself as the largest and the most pre-eminent business aviation airport in the UK1. It is open 07:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and 08:00 to 20:00 at weekends and on bank holidays; closed on Christmas Day and Boxing Day2, and the planning rules are strict: no flying outside 07:00-22:00 on weekdays and 08:00-20:00 at weekends and on bank holidays, except emergencies3. It has one runway, 06/24, 45 m wide; longest take-off run available 2,063 m (runway 24)4. Our Farnborough guide has the details.

Biggin Hill

Biggin Hill lies 15 miles from central London and 12 miles from Canary Wharf5. It is open 06:30 to 23:00 on weekdays and 08:00 to 22:00 at weekends and on public holidays6, and it has runway 03/21, 1,806 m long and 45 m wide6. A practical advantage: no runway slots required5. The rule on flight notification: all movements without a flight plan must be pre-notified to air traffic control7.

Luton

Luton is open 24 hours8 and has one runway, 2.16 km long9. It is a busy airline airport as well: a fully coordinated (Level 3) airport, so slots are allocated by the UK slot coordinator (ACL)10. At night there is no night ban (24-hour licence), but a night quota period 23:30-06:00 local time with movement limits and quota counts; older-generation jets are not allowed at night11. Of the three, it is the one open around the clock.

Choosing the airport

The right airport depends on the time of day, the size of the aircraft and where in London you are going. A morning departure from the south-east may suit Biggin Hill; a late-night arrival points to Luton. We check the hours, slots and runway for your aircraft at each airport before you see a quote.

Questions we are asked

Which London airport is open at night?

Of the three on this page, Luton: it is open 24 hours8, with night noise rules. Farnborough and Biggin Hill close at night.

Does Archē Executive operate the flight?
