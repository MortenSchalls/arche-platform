Private flights to and from New York

New York's private flights use airports outside Manhattan, each with its own rules. How Teterboro and Westchester County (White Plains) compare.

Draft · waiting for approval

Teterboro

Teterboro, in New Jersey, has a firm weight rule: no aircraft over 100,000 lb actual gross weight may land, take off or taxi; heavier-certified aircraft must keep their operating weight at 100,000 lb or less; exceptions need the Airport Manager's prior permission1. Jets also need the airport's permission: no jet aircraft may operate without the Airport Manager's approval (a signed Permission to Operate under the Quiet Flying Program)2. US customs is on site, open 07:30–23:15, seven days a week3. At night there is a voluntary restraint of non-essential flights from 23:00 to 06:00 local, not a mandatory curfew2. Our Teterboro guide has the details.

Westchester County (White Plains)

Westchester County Airport is about 5 miles east of White Plains and about 30 miles north of New York City4. At night there is a voluntary request not to fly between midnight and 06:30 every day, not a mandatory curfew5. The county runs six noise programmes, including a voluntary restraint from flying, a programme for high-noise events of 90 dBA or more, no intersection take-offs, and limits on maintenance run-ups and reverse thrust5.

US customs is available here too, and the same customs rule applies as at Teterboro: customs clears passenger flights only, no cargo6.

Choosing the airport

Teterboro suits most business jets heading to Manhattan, within its weight limit. For trips to the northern suburbs, Westchester County can be closer. We check the aircraft weight, the customs times and the night rules for your flight before you see a quote.

Flights within the United States

Flights between two US cities have their own rule: a foreign civil aircraft may take on passengers or cargo for compensation at one place in the United States for another place in the United States only if specifically authorised under 49 U.S.C. § 40109(g), or under regulations for foreign aircraft leased or chartered without crew to US air carriers7. Questions we are asked

Is there a curfew at New York's private jet airports?

Not a mandatory one at these two: Teterboro has a voluntary restraint of non-essential flights from 23:00 to 06:00 local, not a mandatory curfew2, and Westchester County has a voluntary request not to fly between midnight and 06:30 every day, not a mandatory curfew5.

Does Archē Executive operate the flight?

No. We are a broker. A licensed operator flies every trip, and it is named in your quote and charter agreement before you confirm.
