Private flights to and from Bangkok

Three airports can serve a private trip to Bangkok and the east coast: Don Mueang and Suvarnabhumi near the city, and U-Tapao near Rayong and Pattaya. How they compare, from the Thailand Aeronautical Information Publication.

Don Mueang

Don Mueang lies 12 NM north-east of Bangkok1. It is open 24 hours1, with customs and immigration open 24 hours1, and it has two runways; the longest, 03L/21R, is 3,700 m long and 60 m wide1. It is run by Airports of Thailand Public Company Limited (AOT)1. Its AIP lists a handling agent for private aircraft only.

Suvarnabhumi

Suvarnabhumi lies 25 km east of Bangkok2. It is open 24 hours2, with customs and immigration open 24 hours2, and it has three runways; the two longest, 01/19 and 02L/20R, are each 4,000 m long and 60 m wide2. It is the main airline airport for Bangkok, and its AIP names no business aviation terminal, so private passengers are handled through the operator's handling agent.

U-Tapao

U-Tapao lies 34 km west of Rayong3, convenient for Pattaya and the eastern seaboard. It is operated by the Royal Thai Navy3. It is open 24 hours3, with customs and immigration open 24 hours3, and it has one runway, 18/36, 3,505 m long and 60 m wide3. For trips to the east of Bangkok it can save time on the road.

Domestic flights in Thailand

Flights between two Thai airports are domestic flights. Internationally, most countries prohibit cabotage for hire, and some also bar carrying non-paying passengers between two of their own airports4, so for a leg such as Bangkok to Phuket we check the operator's permission for that leg first. Our cabotage guide explains why.

Questions we are asked

Which Bangkok airport do private jets use?

It depends on the trip. Don Mueang, which lies 12 NM north-east of Bangkok1, lists a handling agent for private aircraft; Suvarnabhumi and U-Tapao are alternatives.

Does Archē Executive operate the flight?

No. We are a broker. A licensed operator flies every trip, and it is named in your quote and charter agreement before you confirm.
