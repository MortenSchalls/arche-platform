Private flights in North America

The United States has clear federal rules for charter. What they mean for your trip, and our guides to New York.

Draft · waiting for approval

Domestic flights in the United States

Flights between two US airports have their own rule: a foreign civil aircraft may take on passengers or cargo for compensation at one place in the United States for another place in the United States only if specifically authorised under 49 U.S.C. § 40109(g), or under regulations for foreign aircraft leased or chartered without crew to US air carriers1 (49 U.S.C. § 41703(c)1). In practice, domestic legs are flown by US operators. We check the operator for every leg before you see a quote.

What a broker must tell you

US rules for charter brokers are written down: in the US a broker's adverts, including web pages, must say it is a broker and not the airline in control of the aircraft2. A US broker must give certain disclosures before the contract for a flight is signed3, and one of them is the corporate name of the operator in operational control of the aircraft3. We follow the same standard everywhere we work.

Taxes

Charter flights in the United States carry federal tax: the IRS states the percentage tax as 7.5% of amounts paid for taxable air transport of persons4. A separate US tax applies to international air transport that begins or ends in the United States4. Your quote states which taxes are included.

New York

New York's private flights use airports outside Manhattan. At Teterboro, no aircraft over 100,000 lb actual gross weight may land, take off or taxi; heavier-certified aircraft must keep their operating weight at 100,000 lb or less; exceptions need the Airport Manager's prior permission5. Westchester County is about 5 miles east of White Plains and about 30 miles north of New York City6. Our New York guide compares them.

Any aircraft, any route

We are a broker, not an airline, and we have no fixed routes. Tell us where you want to go and when, and we find aircraft from licensed operators, check each operator and name it in your quote before you confirm. Any aircraft, any route, upon availability.
