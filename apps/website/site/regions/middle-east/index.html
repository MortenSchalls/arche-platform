Slots and permission in Dubai

Both of Dubai's international airports need a slot for every private flight. Dubai International is a fully slot-coordinated (IATA level 3) airport: no flight without a slot from the slot coordinator (Airport Coordination Limited, ACL) and landing permission from the Dubai Civil Aviation Authority1. At Dubai World Central, schedule clearance (a slot) from the schedules facilitator (Airport Coordination Limited, ACL) is needed before any flight, plus prior permission from the Dubai Civil Aviation Authority; requests are made through the operator's handling agent2. And the largest aircraft (code F) must apply for schedule clearance at least 72 hours before arrival2.

Choosing the airport

Dubai International is 2.5 nautical miles east of Dubai1. Dubai World Central has runway 12/30, 4,500 m long and 60 m wide3. The airport chart shows a VIP Terminal3. We compare both for your times, the slot available and where you are going.

Routings across the region

Long-haul private trips through the Gulf often cross several countries, each with its own overflight and landing permission. The operator applies for each one; we plan the routing with it before you see a quote.
