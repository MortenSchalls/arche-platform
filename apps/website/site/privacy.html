Who we are

Archē Executive arranges private jet charters as agent for our clients. We are responsible for the personal data you give us on this website and when you book with us.

What we collect

  • From the quote form: your route, dates, number of passengers, your name and how to reach you (phone, WhatsApp, email or LINE).
  • From the call-back form: your phone number.
  • When you book: the details the operator and the authorities need for the flight, such as passenger names and travel documents. We ask for these only when you confirm a trip.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking on this website.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request and prepare quotes.
  • To arrange and look after your flight once you book.
  • To meet our legal duties, for example anti-money-laundering and sanctions checks.

We never sell your data and never use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who sees it

Only the Archē Executive team handling your request. When you book, we share what is needed with the aircraft operator, ground handlers and authorities for that flight, and with our payment provider. Our systems are hosted by service providers that process data on our instructions, including in Singapore.

How long we keep it

Requests that do not lead to a booking: up to 12 months. Booking records: as long as the law requires us to keep them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Write to privacy@archeaviation.com. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live, such as Singapore's PDPC or Thailand's PDPC.
