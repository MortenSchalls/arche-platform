Privacy notice

Arche Executive Pte. Ltd. · 20 Collyer Quay, #09-01, Singapore 049319 · Last updated 30 October 2026

Who we are

Archē Executive arranges private jet charters as agent for our clients. We are responsible for the personal data you give us on this website and when you book with us.

What we collect

  • From the quote form: your route, dates, number of passengers, your name and how to reach you (phone, WhatsApp or email).
  • From the call-back form: your phone number.
  • From the empty-leg alerts sign-up: your email and the routes you fly. If you tick the box on the quote form, we also send you empty-leg alerts and occasional news; this box is never ticked for you.
  • If you answer it, whether you have flown privately before.
  • When you book: the details the operator and the authorities need for the flight, such as passenger names and travel documents. We ask for these only when you confirm a trip.

We do not use cookies, advertising trackers or outside analytics services on this website.

Website statistics

To improve the website and to follow up interest from organisations, our own website records each visit without cookies and without storing anything in your browser: the page, the website or campaign you came from, your country and city (worked out from your network address, which we do not store), the type of device, the sections you reached and what you used, such as the quote form, call, WhatsApp or email. We cannot tell who you are from these records.

Links we send to an organisation can carry a short code. If you open such a link, we know that the link sent to your organisation was opened, and what was used on the site. We use this only to follow up the conversation we started, never to build a profile of a person.

If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, nothing is recorded. Statistics are kept for 13 months and seen only by the Archē Executive team.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request and prepare quotes.
  • To arrange and look after your flight once you book.
  • To meet our legal duties, for example anti-money-laundering and sanctions checks.
  • To send empty-leg alerts and news, only if you asked for them.

For visitors in the European Union, the United Kingdom and similar laws, the legal grounds are: taking steps you asked for before a contract and carrying out the contract (quotes and bookings); our legal duties (checks and records); our legitimate interest in running and improving the website (statistics, which carry no cookies and no stored network address); and your consent (alerts and news), which you can withdraw at any time.

We never sell your data and never use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who sees it

Only the Archē Executive team handling your request. When you book, we share what is needed with the aircraft operator, ground handlers and authorities for that flight, and with our payment provider. Our website, database and email are run by service providers that process data only on our instructions. They may store or process it outside the country you are in, including in Singapore and the United States. When data leaves your country, we rely on these providers' data processing terms to keep it protected to a standard comparable to the law that applies to you.

We protect your data with encrypted connections, access limited to the people who need it, sign-in for every team member and regular encrypted backups.

How long we keep it

Requests that do not lead to a booking: up to 12 months. Booking records: as long as the law requires us to keep them. Empty-leg alerts and news: until you unsubscribe. Website statistics: 13 months.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Where the law that applies to you gives you further rights, such as to receive your data in a portable form, to restrict our use of it or to object to it, you can use them the same way. Write to privacy@archeaviation.com. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live, such as Singapore's PDPC, Thailand's PDPC or the authority in your EU or UK country.

Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at the same address.

Children

This website is not meant for children. We arrange travel for children only with a parent or guardian who books for them.

Changes

When we change this notice, we change the date at the top.
