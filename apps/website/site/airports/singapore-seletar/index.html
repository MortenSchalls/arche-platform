Airport guide · Singapore

Seletar Airport, Singapore

Seletar Airport in the north of Singapore has its own Business Aviation Centre. What a private flight here involves, checked against the official Singapore Aeronautical Information Publication.

What it is like to fly from Seletar

Seletar is run by Changi Airport Group1 and sits 14.6 km north of the city centre1. Private passengers do not go through an airline terminal: non-scheduled flights clear customs and immigration at the Seletar Business Aviation Centre1. Customs and immigration are open 24 hours1.

Every flight is handled locally: every arriving and departing aircraft must appoint a licensed ground handling agent1. The operator of your flight appoints the handler; we make sure the handler has your passenger details, catering and transport in good time.

Times and the night curfew

The airport has a night curfew: no flights between 22:00 and 07:00 Singapore time (14:00–23:00 UTC), except medical evacuation and emergency flights1. A late-evening arrival or an early departure therefore needs planning. If your timing falls inside the curfew, we adjust the schedule with you before anything is booked; Changi is an alternative only for larger aircraft.

Fuel is available 07:30–22:00 Monday to Friday and 08:30–17:30 on weekends and public holidays, Singapore time1. The operator plans the fuel for your schedule around these hours.

Which aircraft can use it

Seletar has one runway, 03/21, 1,836 m long and 46 m wide1. Parking is limited by stand size: the largest stands take aircraft up to a Boeing 757-200 without winglets1. Whether a particular aircraft can use the runway at the weight your trip needs is the operator's calculation; we have it confirmed before you receive a quote.

There is also a noise rule: subsonic jets need a noise certificate to ICAO Annex 16, Volume I, Chapter 3 or equivalent1. Every operator we propose is checked against it before you receive a quote.

Seletar or Changi

For most business jets there is no choice: Changi is not available to non-scheduled civil aircraft of 40 seats or fewer except in special circumstances2. Seletar is the airport for private jets in Singapore. Changi becomes an option for larger aircraft, such as converted airliners used for groups, and then with its own timing rule: larger non-scheduled aircraft shall not plan to arrive between 17:00 and 23:59 local time2. We check both airports for every Singapore trip and tell you which one works for your aircraft and times.

How current this is

Everything on this page comes from the Singapore Aeronautical Information Publication; the 2026 amendments published so far list no change for Seletar beyond its charts3. Airport rules can change at short notice. The operator and its handler confirm the current rules for your flight before departure.

Questions we are asked

Can a private jet land at Seletar at night?

No, except medical evacuation and emergency flights: no flights between 22:00 and 07:00 Singapore time (14:00–23:00 UTC), except medical evacuation and emergency flights1.

Where do private passengers clear immigration at Seletar?

At the airport itself: non-scheduled flights clear customs and immigration at the Seletar Business Aviation Centre1. Customs and immigration are open 24 hours1.

Which is better for a private flight, Seletar or Changi?

For a business jet, Seletar: Changi is not available to non-scheduled civil aircraft of 40 seats or fewer except in special circumstances2. Changi is an option for larger aircraft with more seats, outside its evening arrival window. We check both for every trip.
