Airport guide · London

Farnborough Airport, London

Farnborough, south-west of London, calls itself the largest and the most pre-eminent business aviation airport in the UK5. What its rules mean for your trip.

Opening hours

Farnborough is not a twenty-four-hour airport. It is open 07:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and 08:00 to 20:00 at weekends and on bank holidays; closed on Christmas Day and Boxing Day1. The planning rules behind these hours are strict: no flying outside 07:00-22:00 on weekdays and 08:00-20:00 at weekends and on bank holidays, except emergencies2. A late return from a meeting or an early departure therefore needs planning; if your times fall outside the hours, we look at another London airport with you before anything is booked.

Built for business aviation

The airport is run by Farnborough Airport Ltd3. Its growth is capped by planning limits: planning limits of 50,000 movements a year, of which no more than 8,900 at weekends and bank holidays3.

There is also a rule on noise on the ground: no use of aircraft auxiliary power units between 22:30 and 06:303.

Which aircraft can use it

Farnborough has one runway, 06/24, 45 m wide; longest take-off run available 2,063 m (runway 24)4. Whether a particular aircraft can use the runway at the weight your trip needs is the operator's calculation, and we have it confirmed before you receive a quote.

Other London airports

London has several airports used by private jets, each with different hours and rules. Our London page compares the main ones.

How current this is

This page uses the airport's own published information and the local planning authority. Airport rules can change at short notice. The operator and its handling agent confirm the current rules for your flight before departure.

Questions we are asked

Can I fly into Farnborough at night?

No. No flying outside 07:00-22:00 on weekdays and 08:00-20:00 at weekends and on bank holidays, except emergencies2.

Is Farnborough open on public holidays?

Yes, with weekend hours, except over Christmas: it is open 07:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and 08:00 to 20:00 at weekends and on bank holidays; closed on Christmas Day and Boxing Day1.
