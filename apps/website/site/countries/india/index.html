Private flights to and from India

Every private or charter flight to India needs the authority's permission first. What the rules are, how far ahead to plan, and what they mean for your trip.

Draft · waiting for approval

Permission to land

The authority is the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA)1. The rule: non-scheduled flights into, from or over India need prior DGCA approval, applied for 3 working days ahead for flights carrying traffic1. Overflight is enforced: overflying aircraft must quote a DGCA clearance or may be made to land1.

Domestic legs

An international flight cannot be used as a domestic flight: international flights may not pick up passengers in India and set them down elsewhere in India1. If your trip includes a leg between two Indian cities, we check that the operator is allowed to fly it before you see a quote.

Airports

For international flights: first landing and final departure at an international aerodrome1; and immigration and customs clearance on the General Declaration is required before departure1.

The main airports add their own rules. At Delhi, non-scheduled flights need a valid Delhi Approach Arrival Clearance Number (DACN), applied for from the airport's operations control centre2, and international non-scheduled flights must get night parking approval from DIAL's flight data unit at least 5 days ahead and bring their own tow bar2. At Mumbai, requests for international movements at least 72 hours ahead, domestic at least 12 hours ahead3, and no general aviation between 08:00-10:00 and 17:00-19:30 local time daily (0230-0430 and 1130-1400 UTC); only VIP and scheduled flights then3.

Who applies for the permit

The operator that flies your trip applies, usually through its handling agent or a flight-permit service. You do not need to deal with the authority yourself. What you can do is give us final names, passport details and times early, because a permit is issued for a given aircraft, schedule and passenger list, and late changes can mean a new application.

How we plan a private flight to or from India

Tell us where you are flying from or to, when, and how many people are travelling. We find aircraft from licensed operators, check each operator against our safety standard before you see a quote, and ask the operator to apply for the permits early, because the authority's answer decides the schedule. Questions we are asked

Can I fly from Delhi to Mumbai on the same private jet I arrived on?

Not as a domestic charter by a foreign operator: international flights may not pick up passengers in India and set them down elsewhere in India1. We find an operator allowed to fly that leg.

Does Archē Executive operate the flight?

No. We are a broker. A licensed operator flies every trip, and it is named in your quote and charter agreement before you confirm.
