## 1. Where we already lead
- **Sourced facts:** every fact on our content pages carries its source and the date it was checked. No peer does this, regional or global. ASA quotes prices it says elsewhere it cannot publish honestly. ACS's Thailand guide calls Pattaya an island. ASA calls the Lineage 1000 "a light jet".
- **A published operator safety standard,** enforced in the portal. No regional peer names an audit level. ASA only checks that documents are in date. Singapore Air Charter says "100% Government & Corporate Trusted" without a basis.
- **Broker honesty:** no fleet claims; every page ends "any aircraft, any route, upon availability". Singapore Air Charter has an "Our Fleet" section and claims "500+ aircraft". ASA and ACS use "Asia's leading" and similar claims.
- **Languages:** English, Thai, Chinese and Japanese on one domain. ASA, VR Luxe and MJets are English only. Chapman has 11 languages but not Thai or Japanese. Only ACS matches us, and it does so on separate country sites.
- **Design:** a calm charcoal and silver palette with Prata and Cinzel headings, real photography, the watermark and the compass rose. It looks premium and consistent, and no regional peer comes close: ASA and Singapore Air Charter use template designs.
- **Conversion tools:**
  - a quote widget in the hero (one way, return and multi-leg);
  - an aircraft finder with to-scale cabin drawings;
  - WhatsApp, call-back within 15 minutes, and email;
  - a five-step "how it works".
- **Hidden until switch-on:** the test website is out of search engines until launch, with three layers of protection.

## 3. User friendliness: what was measured on our home page
- **Speed:** about 2 MB over 37 requests; loaded locally in 0.3 s on desktop and 0.6 s on a phone. That is good. Keep new pages static.
- **No sideways scrolling** at 390 px on any page.
- **Fixed today:**
  - Hero form labels were 9 px. They are now 11 px.
  - Form fields were 19 px high. They are now 40 px on phones, with 16 px text so phones don't zoom.
  - The passenger buttons were 22 px. They are now 40 px on phones.
  - Footer links now have larger tap areas.
  - Fine print is now at least 13 px.
- **Still small (by design):** the uppercase section labels at 12.5 px and the language links at 29 px high. They are readable and meet the 24 px minimum target size, but larger would be kinder to older clients. Your call.
- **Contrast:** body text is light grey on charcoal and passes. The silver buttons with dark text pass. No failing body text was found. The automated tool flagged some cards with image or gradient backgrounds; on screen they read well.
- **Layout gaps:** three areas on the home page have large empty space:
  - under the service cards;
  - around the "How it works" label;
  - after the aircraft finder.

  Tightening them shortens the page, which runs to about 12,000 px on desktop and 16,000 px on phones.
- **The phone placeholder +65 0000 0000** shows in five places until the Singapore number exists. It must not go live.
