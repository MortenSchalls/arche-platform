Enforced in the portal (src-modules/opsafety.js); manual v3.8 Module 8 updated; academies rebuilt; charter confirmation label updated. ## 1. What changed at ARGUS
- **Gold Plus no longer exists.** It meant ARGUS Gold plus an ARGUS on-site audit; findings could still be open. In 2009–10 ARGUS also accepted an IS-BAO Stage 1 audit in its place. ARGUS has published no notice of when or why it was dropped. The only trace is an undated chart on its auditing-services page.
- **The current ladder** is Gold, Gold Certified, Platinum and Platinum Elite:
  - **Gold** is a records review, widened in Oct 2025 to a remote audit. It "will no longer be offered after May 2027".
  - **Gold Certified** was introduced in 2025. ARGUS's dated post of 30 Apr 2026 says it is "performed remotely in one day with one auditor"; its undated product page speaks of an "onsite audit component". Treat it as remote unless ARGUS confirms otherwise in writing.
  - **Platinum** is a two-day on-site audit, renewed every two years.
  - **Platinum Elite** needs consecutive Platinum audits with no significant findings, plus reviews every six months.
- **Conclusion:** Gold Certified is not a like-for-like replacement for Gold Plus. To keep the standard (an independent on-site audit), the ARGUS route must be Platinum or Platinum Elite.

## 2. The other audits
- **WYVERN Wingman** (Standard, 3rd edition) goes well beyond regulation:
  - two pilots on every flight;
  - captain minimums of 3,500 hours total, 200 on type and an ATP licence;
  - a working safety management system;
  - five years clear of accidents, incidents and sanctions;
  - a 2–3 day on-site audit every 24 months.

  Wingman PRO adds a fully effective safety management system. WYVERN "Registered" is not an audit: it is data the operator submits.
- **IS-BAO (IBAC)** is an independent third-party audit, renewed every two or three years, and IBAC calls it the "only ICAO-recognized" business-aviation standard. It has three stages:
  - Stage 1: the safety management system is in place.
  - Stage 2: risks are managed effectively.
  - Stage 3: safety management is fully part of the business.
- **ACSF IAS** covers US operators only. BARS is for the mining and resources sector. IS-BAH covers ground handlers, not flights.
- **No official equivalence** exists between these programmes. Any mapping is Archē's own judgement.

## 3. What peers publish (about 30 brokers and bodies checked)
- **No broker anywhere publishes a minimum above ours.** The only published minimum found is Paramount's ARGUS Gold, and only for US flights.
- **The European and Asian brokers name no level at all.** These are ACS, Air Partner, PrivateFly/FXAIR, LunaJets, Chapman Freeborn, Global Charter, Victor and L'VOYAGE; they promise "audits" or their own standards.
- **The industry bodies set only the legal minimum:** the Air Charter Association code, the UK CAA and EBAA.
- **Where the best peers go further, in fixed requirements rather than ratings:**
  - Two pilots on every flight: LunaJets and Chapman Freeborn ("single-pilot operations are categorically excluded").
  - Crew minimums, at Stratos Jets: captain 3,000 hours total and 250 on type.
  - Insurance:
    - Stratos requires USD 50M on jets and USD 25M on turboprops, plus its own USD 50M cover.
    - Sentient requires USD 50M and adds USD 100M of its own.
    - Chapman Freeborn guarantees at least USD 150M on US flights.
  - Accident history: five years clear at Stratos and in WYVERN Wingman.
  - A safety report on every trip, using ARGUS TripCHEQ or WYVERN PASS: Paramount, PrivateFly and L'VOYAGE.
  - Exceptions disclosed to the client, who decides: Stratos and Paramount.
  - Suspending an operator after an incident until reviewed: Jets.com (trade press).
  - A named safety lead: FXAIR and Sentient.

## 4. Asia-Pacific and the Middle East
- **ARGUS is not a practical requirement here.** No current ARGUS-rated operator was found; the Asian awards found date from 2011–2013.
- **Operators found with a current audit:**
  - **WYVERN Wingman:** Phenix Jet, TAG Aviation (Cayman and San Marino AOCs), Executive Aviation Taiwan, ExecuJet Australia, IRM India, Qatar Executive and Gulf Wings.
  - **IS-BAO:** Sino Jet (Stage 3), Metrojet (Stage 3), Qatar Executive and Falconer (Stage 2).
- **Many "Asian" operators fly on Cayman, San Marino, Guam or Malta AOCs.** The audit must belong to the AOC that flies the trip.
- **Consequence:** most regional trips will use the director-approval route. That route must be fast and well documented, not a loophole.

## 5. Proposed rule
**Tier 1, accepted without director approval.** The audit must be current, checked in the auditor's own registry or on its certificate for the AOC that flies the trip, and recorded with its expiry date:
- ARGUS Platinum or Platinum Elite
- WYVERN Wingman or Wingman PRO
- IS-BAO Stage 2 or Stage 3

**Tier 2, director approval, and the client told in writing before signature:**
- ARGUS Gold or Gold Certified, IS-BAO Stage 1, WYVERN Registered, another audit, or no audit.
- The unrated-operator checks in manual 8.3 apply in full:
  - AOC confirmed with the authority;
  - insurance confirmed by the insurer;
  - IS-BAO or the Archē questionnaire;
  - no adverse history;
  - two references.
- Plus crew minimums confirmed by the operator in writing: captain 3,000 hours total and 250 on type; first officer 1,000 hours total.

**For every operator, in both tiers:**
- Two pilots on every jet flight. No single-pilot commercial jet flights.
- Five years without a fatal accident, serious incident or regulatory sanction, unless it is explained and the director approves. This was 36 months.
- Passenger liability insurance of at least USD 50M for jets and USD 25M for turboprops. Confirm with our insurance broker that this is normal in Asia before it is fixed.
- Any accident or serious incident suspends the operator until the director has reviewed it.
- On every booking: the audit status and expiry date are re-checked at the source and saved on the trip. US rules forbid misstating an audit (14 CFR 295.50(b)(7)).
- **Later, with the safety-rating subscription (owner task "Subscribe to operator safety ratings"):** a TripCHEQ or WYVERN PASS report on every flight.

**Why this keeps or raises the standard:**
- Every Tier 1 route includes an independent on-site or third-party audit, as Gold Plus did.
- IS-BAO Stage 2 is one step above the Stage 1 that ARGUS itself accepted for Gold Plus.
- Remote-only audits move down to director approval.
- The fixed minimums for every operator come from the strictest peers and from WYVERN Wingman.
